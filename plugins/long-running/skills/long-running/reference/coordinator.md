# The coordinator

| Input | Do, in this turn |
|---|---|
| An order, in an `owner` record | `drive order add "<his words, verbatim>" --from slack:<channel>/<ts> --thread <ts> --parts "<part>"...` for each order, one part per deliverable (stack, design or question; omit `--parts` for one), `--milestone "<name>"` for the outcome it moves (reusing an existing milestone, or `drive order set <o> --milestone` later so it does not sit under "No milestone" on Plan), `--project "<name>" --workstream "<name>"` reusing pairs from `drive order unfiled` (`drive order set <o> --project "<name>" --workstream "<name>"` files it later so it does not sit under "Unfiled" on Now and Work), and `--due` for when he hears next (`drive order add` refuses `--due` without `--thread`). By [Design first](design.md), a part with a tricky design starts with a design lane; its builder may build recommended options while his questions are open, but opens no PR until he answers. Start a background job for each part in his order with `--part`; if `drive job add` prints a job already running or waiting on that part, start no agent, run `drive job set <it> --state stopped`, and leave the part on the existing job. A part asking for cards or a summary is delivered by those cards and builds no fix before his pick, by [Scope](scope.md). Every order—past-due, one-line or session close—gets a job; update parts found later with `drive order set <o> --parts`. An order to land a change with an open design card lands nothing: send a `report` to his order's thread to redraw that card, and land on its `FREE` row once he answers, by [Design first](design.md). |
| A ruling, in an `owner` record: any message of his that says how the drive, its two sessions, cards or messages should work, a correction included | Handle on arrival in the same turn, unasked. Record it with `answer_add` unless the record's `ruling` attr already names its answer, then run `drive answer` before anything else, as the `answer` row says, so superseded cards stop asking and changed PRs do not land as they stand. The communicator owns the skill rewrite on its ack. Start a reader job to find every live job the ruling touches; when it returns, send each job his words verbatim, their time and what they mean for that job via `SendMessage`, rather than the answer id alone. |
| A question of his, in an `owner` record, such as one a card's `ask` record does not answer | Send a `report` of what you have now within 5 to 10 seconds; if reading takes more than one call, run a background reader job and send its result in a second `report`. When an unanswered card of his blocks the work he asks about, name each blocking card's permalink and post time in the `report` so the handler redraws it with its buttons in his thread, rather than saying it is parked without the card or proceeding on its recommended option. |
| An `answer` record | Call `answer_add` with the card's question as title and the tapped option or his words verbatim as body. Before starting anything, run `drive answer <its answer id> --words "<his words, verbatim>" --link <the permalink of his message or the card he tapped>` to list every open question card, PR awaiting a tap, other open PR and merged PR whose job is still running, and mark each after reading its question or body against his words (`drive answer` refuses until every listed item is marked, and refuses partial stack marks): `--stands <key>` if unchanged; `--superseded <key>=stop` (ending a stopped job's agent, or `drive job end <j>` for a remote lane) or `<key>=<j>` to move its work to live job `<j>`; `--reopened <key>=<j>` for an open or unshipped merged PR his words change, briefing `<j>` with his words; and `--others-stand` for the rest. For `maps=none`, act on his words if they settle the question; if they leave it open, send a follow-up `ask` with `--attr waits=<that card's waits id>` to redraw that card in place before resuming any job. When his words or ruling send an unanswered card back for rework, immediately send an `ask` with `--attr ask=<the card's kept file>`, `--attr waits=<its waits id>`, `--attr reworking=<what is being redone, and when it is back>` and `--attr job=<j>` so the card shows it is being reworked while keeping its buttons; if he taps it meanwhile, his pick stands and the redo stops, by [the question card](messages.md#the-shapes). Pass an answer to a design lane's question verbatim into the builder's brief or handoff, by [Design first](design.md); the design job is `done` once `drive status` lists no open questions under it, and work built ahead opens its PR on the `FREE` row. Otherwise resume its job (`SendMessage` and `drive job set <j> --state running`, or `drive job resume` for a remote lane; the communicator resumes drive tooling jobs at his tap). For `undone` within ten minutes of his tap, treat the answer as never given: call `answer_add` with `Undone: <label>. Never given; the card waits for his answer.`, call `answer_supersede` on the earlier answer, tell any resumed job or builder via `SendMessage` (`drive job resume` for a remote lane) to drop work built on it and wait, and run `drive job set <j> --state waiting --card <the card's permalink>` (plus `--waits <the card's waits id>` if it built ahead of a design card) so the watch holds the card open until his next tap supersedes it. |
| A `refused` record | Send the refusal verbatim back to the job named in its `job` attr (or the design job for a design fork) via `SendMessage` or `drive job resume <j> "<the refusal>"` so it fixes the `ASK` and its row posts the new `ask`. |
| A message he typed in this terminal | Send an `owner` record of his words to `communicator@<drive>`, which acks it, marks him present and returns it as his message. |
| A job finished, or a `JOB …` event | Follow the matching row below for its one-line result. Read the full return with `drive job report <j>` only when a row requires it, and reference `drive job report <j>` in a handler's `report` body instead of copying the facts. |
| A watch event | Handle each line by its row below (drive tooling lines go to the communicator). `TAP`, `DUE`, `ANSWER`, `JOB`, `LANE`, `EVENT catch-up` and `EVENT unheard` arrive immediately; other lines batch with the next of those or after 5 minutes. Run `drive status <order, job or PR>` for the named rows only, reserving a full `drive status` for start, restart or compaction so Fable does not reread unchanged state. |
| An incident: his order with incident priority, an alert he sent in an `owner` record, or an alert in a held channel the drive's own release or change caused, or a drive release that fails partway through its rollout | Follow [reference/incidents.md](incidents.md) first. One failing service is one incident with one record and one owning job; join later alerts or questions with `drive order add --joins <o>` and quote cause updates from that record. Run `drive order add --incident --parts fix "first check"` after a release or new build, else `--parts rollback fix cause`, then start all recorded codex jobs on the next message (`drive order add` and `drive order set` refuse an incident without a `fix` part). Start the fix job with apply authority alongside diagnosis: check whether releasing the newest default-branch build fixes it, or roll back to the newest build without the failing change while the cause job owns the record; diagnosis feeds findings to the fix job without gating it. If a release broke the service, freeze the service's other releases in that first message until the fix merges and ships. Announce any blocking card in the incident's thread with what it blocks and the recommended option being built meanwhile, then pass that post to `drive job set --card` with `--told` (`drive job set` refuses an incident card without `--told`). Land a revert only as a normal PR on his tap of a card stating the bug. Send an update at least every 5 minutes until users are unblocked, then once per root-fix step. Ignore any other alert. |
| A record from another session | Reply on cci as its kind asks. Unless an order of his names a PR or bug it hands over, send it back in a `handoff` record with no job and no card, by [Scope](scope.md). |

| Job result | Do |
|---|---|
| `DONE <proof>` | When the order's last job finishes, run `drive order set <o> --proof <proof>` and include the result in the thread's next summary or his catch-up via a `report` with `--attr close=<o>` (space-separated for multiple orders) and `drive job report <j>` in its body: what changed, what it served (his words, or "port of #N landed" linking the old PR), and the proof link. The handler closes the order with `drive order set <o> --state done --record <the report's seq>` after posting (`drive order set` refuses if the record's `close` attr omits `<o>` or if a promised line is still owed). For a remote lane, run `drive job end <j>` in the background. |
| `READY <pr url>...` | For a remote lane, run `drive job end <j>` in the background. Read `drive job report <j>` and check `SERVES:` by [Scope](scope.md); send a drive tooling PR as a `defect` record to `communicator@<drive>`. **A port**: compare against #N with `gh pr diff`; if it does only what #N does, run `drive pr set <url> --port-of <N>` for the `pure port` exemption and land it with a handoff builder and no card. **His order**: verify every step in his order matches code in the PR body's `His words, step by step` list and every design decision in `DESIGN:` is settled by his answer or card (otherwise return it to the job for code or an `ASK` by [Design first](design.md)). If the PR has an open design card, run `drive pr set <url> --waits <card>` for each and draw no card. Otherwise run `drive pr set <url> --tap --serves <o> --design asked:<his answer id or card permalink>` or `--design none` (`drive pr set` refuses if a wait holds or the body fails [Land a change](landing.md#every-pr) checks such as `## Releasable alone`), and send one `ready` record with every URL bottom first, the order's thread and his order's words. **Neither**: send another lane's PR back in a `handoff` record, and run `drive pr set <url> --no-tap --why "<reason>"` to withdraw any open card in place. A PR added to the stack later returns as its own `READY`. |
| `WAITING <what>` | For a remote lane, run `drive job end <j>` in the background and send nothing to him. Start a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) on its open PR's next event; for any other wait, set `drive order set <o> --due` for when to check next. |
| `ASK <path>` | The job saved its block with `drive job ask` (for a remote lane's `JOB … ASK`, run `drive job ask <j>` here and return any refusal via `drive job resume <j> "<the refusal>"`; `drive job ask` refuses a malformed, undrawable or already-settled block and names the fix). Send one `ask` record per question with the question as text, `--attr ask=<the path it printed>`, `--attr job=<j>` and the order's thread (plus `--attr waits=<that card's waits id>` when redoing an unanswered card to clear `Being reworked`). The summary's first line states what was found, while full findings stay in the file behind Explain more; for a design question, state the design in one line for the summary, code or calls per option in Show me, and what his words settle plus each option's changes and costs in the findings, by [Design first](design.md). If the `ASK` supersedes a PR with a drawn tap card, once the `posted` record gives the question card's link, run `drive pr set <pr url> --waits <that link>` yourself to hold the tap card until `FREE`. Resume the job on his `answer` or a `refused` record. |
| `DESIGN <part>`, from a design lane | Read `drive job report <j>`, by [Design first](design.md). For each `Fork: <path>` line saved with `drive job ask`, send one `ask` record with its question as text, `--attr ask=<that path>`, `--attr job=<j>` and the order's thread. Start the part's builder now with the design in its brief's `Plan:`: without forks it returns `READY`; with forks it builds the recommended options and returns `BUILT` with `Decisions that bind` naming each open card and built option. |
| `BUILT <branch>, waits on <cards>` | Run `drive job set <j> --state built --branch <branch> --waits <card>` for each card's waits id or Slack link, and `drive job end <j>` in the background for a remote lane (`drive pr add` refuses a new PR while a card is open). Send nothing to him; start its PR on the `FREE` row. |
| `BLOCKED <what>` | Route the work around the block. Send a broken tool or a drive tooling PR (`BLOCKED no order of his names this`) as a `defect` record to `communicator@<drive>` quoting the refusal; return any other unrequested PR to its lane or turn it into a question card, by [Scope](scope.md). |

| Event | Do |
|---|---|
| `PR … checks failed`, `conflicts with its base`, `changes requested` | Start a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) carrying the event line. When the watch reports `new head`, `checks passed` or `approved`, the PR is green, approved and waiting only on his card: follow the `READY` row. |
| `PR … merged` | Start a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) to install and prove it live, or mark the order done if nothing needs installing. |
| `PR … closed` | End the order's work on it if he closed it; otherwise start a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) to reopen or replace it. |
| `TAP Approve …` | Start a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) to land it (one handoff carries all `TAP Approve` lines of a stack, bottom first), unless the PR still waits on a card or job and lands on `FREE`. |
| `TAP Hold …` | Start no handoff for that PR until he says otherwise. |
| `DUE o<n> "…" was due <time>: <why>` | Read the order's job transcripts first so you never call a job silent, stuck or done unread, set the new time with `--due` (if `why` is no thread, set `drive order set <o> --thread <ts>` first), and send a `report` for that thread with what is late, why and the new time; only his own message withdraws an unposted time via `drive order set <o> --withdrawn <its permalink>` (`drive order set` refuses past times, missing threads or non-owner withdrawal links). While he is away, hold non-incident lines for his catch-up; send incident lines at once by [reference/incidents.md](incidents.md). |
| `ANSWER <id> "…": <n> cards and <m> PRs with no mark, standing, superseded or reopened by it; drive answer <id> lists them` | Run `drive answer <id>` and mark each listed card and PR by the `answer` row so the watch stops repeating the reminder every 10 minutes. |
| `TICK o<n> …` | Send one `report` per thread (his main thread for threadless orders), grouped by feature, covering each listed order's progress since the last line and what is next or that its promised time holds, then run `drive order set <o> --lined` (plus `--due` when promising a new time, which takes effect once the line posts). Send nothing for orders without news. |
| `EVENT catch-up dm=… since=… orders=…` | Send one `report` with no thread for his DM root opening with `*Catch-up since <since>*`, grouped by feature, listing what waits on him last with each card's permalink linked under descriptive words, then run `--lined` on each order; never post it in the thread of the message that brought him back, by [When it goes out](messages.md#when-it-goes-out). |
| `EVENT refused …` | Start a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) to fix the PR's title or body for the reason given, then resend `ready`. Do not rewrite a PR refused because no order names it (land a port without a card, send drive tooling as a `defect` record to `communicator@<drive>`, or return neither by [Scope](scope.md)) or because it waits on a card or job (wait for `FREE`). |
| `EVENT unheard …` | Handle the line's question, pick and link as his `answer` to that card. |
| `ORPHAN o<n> "<part>": <why>` | Resolve every orphan in this turn so it does not repeat every 30 minutes and at `DUE`: start, resume or restart a job, assign the part to a running job already doing the work, draw its card, send an unposted answer from the job's transcript as a `report` to the communicator, process a finished job's result from `drive job report <j>` or `drive job replies <j>`, track an untracked PR with `drive pr add <url> --job <j>`, or remove delivered or withdrawn parts with `drive order set <o> --parts` (or `--state done` or `dropped`). |
| `PR … still red for <n> minutes`, `green and approved with no card drawn for <n> minutes` | Give a red PR a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page), and take a green PR awaiting his tap to the `READY` row (ports and unrequested PRs get no card). If a PR waits on an open card or job first, run `drive pr set <url> --waits <the card's Slack link or waits id, or the job>` to withdraw any drawn Approve card and suppress green alerts until `FREE` (`--no-waits` clears them, and `drive view` and the dashboard still list the PR). |
| `FREE <j> <o>: … nothing else holds it, built on <branch>`, `FREE <pr> <j> <o>: …` | Every card and job holding the job or PR is settled. For a built job, start a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) carrying his answers verbatim to rebuild where his pick differed from the recommended option, open the PR and return `READY`. For a PR, send the same handoff if his pick differed from what was built; otherwise follow `READY` if it awaits his tap, or land it if he approved. Resume an unbuilt waiting job. |
| `JOB <j> <o> STOPPED …` | Read the remote lane's screen (the `jobs` page) and resume it with its next step. |
| `JOB <j> <o> READ unanswered, its lane was told why: …` | If a tool such as `tools/ci bk` or `cci` failed, handle it like a broken tool under `BLOCKED`; an off-list read needs nothing. |
| `LANE <j> <o> died on Sprite …` | Relaunch the remote lane in this turn by running `drive job resume <j> -` in the background with its next step on stdin, or wait to resume it with his answer if it was waiting on one. |
| `WATCH compacted the communicator …` | Do nothing; the watch ran `/compact` in the communicator's Orca terminal. |
| `WATCH … failed` | Do nothing; the watch retries GitHub or Orca and reports again in ten minutes. |
| `WATCH status page stale since …` | Send nothing to him; the watch retries and reports `WATCH status page redrawn …` when Slack accepts it, or send a `defect` record to `communicator@<drive>` if it persists. |
| `WATCH status page redrawn on a new tab: <url>` | Link the new status tab in the next status line to him. |

While he is away, send only `ready` and `ask` records and the incident lines [reference/incidents.md](incidents.md) calls for; hold everything else for his catch-up.

Before naming a tap in a `report`, `ready` or `ask` record, check `drive status <pr>` so you ask only when owed; the plugin hook denies records that ask to tap, approve or hold an already-tapped, merged or closed PR, by [What goes in](messages.md#what-goes-in).
