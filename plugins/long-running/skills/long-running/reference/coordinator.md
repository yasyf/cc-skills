# The coordinator

`drive manual coordinator <word>` prints the rows whose first cell names that word: read the one row a line names, not the whole page.

## Drive verbs as tools

- Run the `drive order`, `drive job` and `drive pr` verbs that add or set, and `drive status`, as tools of this plugin's `drive` MCP server, never in Bash: `order` (`add`, `set`, `posted`, `told`), `job` (`add`, `set`, `brief`), `pr` (`add`, `set`), `status` and `search_plan`.
- One call of a tool carries every op the turn needs from it, in order, each op one verb with its flags as fields named as in `drive manual verbs`, dashes as underscores, such as `pr` with ops `[{set: {url: "<url>", serves: "o4", design: "none"}}, {set: {url: "<url 2>", waits: ["<card>"]}}]`.
- An op after an `add` that names no `id` acts on what that add made, and a `job` `add` whose `order` holds `order add`'s fields adds the order too.
- A call checks every op before any runs and prints one line per op; an op the check refuses runs none of them, and the call names every refused op with its fix, so fix each and send the whole call again; `dry_run: true` runs each op through its verb's own checks, refused as the call would be, prints the commands and keeps nothing.
- The rows below name each verb as its `drive` command. `drive answer`, `drive card`, `drive job ask`, `report`, `replies`, `launch`, `resume` and `end`, `drive place` and `drive manual` stay in Bash.
- Send an `owner` or `defect` record with the `record` tool, and a `report` or `ask` with the cci `post` tool, since its `record_lines` hook checks them and `cci post` in Bash skips it.

## The top and each sub-coordinator

- A drive can run one sub-coordinator per project beside the top coordinator, each a session of its own bound with `drive start <drive> --role coordinator --project <project>` (the `start` page). It holds the coordinator role, so every rule and row on this page and every hook that checks that role binds it, for its project's orders.
- A sub-coordinator owns the orders filed under its project, the jobs and PRs serving them, never a job of another order only carrying one of them, and lands and installs its project's changes. Its own watch, `drive watch --project <project>`, hears only those, its GitHub and release reads, lane mail, ticks, promised times and `ORPHAN` lines included, and its lines arrive at `<lane>@<drive>`, `<lane>` being the project's name lowercased with each run of other characters a dash. It posts with `--lane <lane>`: its `report` records go to `communicator@<drive>` directly, its `defect` records to the meta session, and his answers to its cards come back to its lane.
- The top hears orders filed under no project or under one with no sub-coordinator yet, every Slack channel the drive holds with the alerts and releases they carry, and the `ANSWER` lines; its watch alone draws the status page and compacts the communicator. It alone starts, approves and rolls back production cuts, one at a time, since one cut carries every project's changes. His words: "the top starts, approves and rolls back production cuts, one at a time".
- The `landing_guard` hook refuses `ci release start`, `resume`, `approve` and `rollback` and a release build's rerun or unblock from a sub-coordinator's session and the agents it starts, naming the top's session; `--plan`, `--dry-run`, `--help`, `status` and `watch` still run. When a change of its project needs a cut his order or pick covers, the sub-coordinator sends a `release` record to `root@<drive>`, a kind the top's subscription wakes on, naming the merged changes the cut must carry and, in backticks, the one `ci release start` or `hotfix` with the `--order` of his answer, or else asking the top to put that release to him, by [Between the sessions](records.md), and the top starts it in one call on the record's order, `drive job add builder --order <o> --goal "<one line>" --release "<the one ci release start or hotfix, --order <his answer>>"`, which starts the release from the top's checkout, prints its build link and writes the job's brief, then starts the job's agent on that brief by [After merge](landing.md#after-merge). The hook refuses every other `ci release start` and `hotfix` from a drive session, its agents or a Sprite lane. A job with a `Release:` line is the top's on any order, its watch lines and agent the top's, and a sub-coordinator is refused adding, briefing or starting one, naming the top.
- An event is stamped as it is written with the lane hearing its order then, so lines from before a binding stay the top's. A job's lines, his answer on its card and a handler's `handoff` naming it go to the session hearing its order, so when its order moves to another coordinator session, a sub-coordinator bound for its project or a refile, the job moves with it and nothing is stopped, by his words "dont kill any existing handlers or workers just reparent them": a Sprite or Orca lane runs on and its mail reaches the new session's watch, and an agent another session started takes `drive job resume <job> <the event>` from the new session, which hands it to a fresh agent there with what the old one left, never a `SendMessage` relayed through the old session. A job whose agent a `SendMessage` woke after its last line reads `resumed <when>` in `drive status` and `drive job replies` until its next line, so no session relays to work already running.
- When an `owner` record carries an order that belongs under a sub-coordinator's project, the top sends the record on with the `record` tool's `to: "<lane>"` and adds no order or job for it, since that order's lines would reach only the sub-coordinator. `drive status` lists each sub-coordinator with its project, lane, session, Orca place and whether its watch runs; the `record` tool refuses a lane no session reads, naming those that are.

## Before you act

1. When a line names an order, job or PR, read only those with `drive status <id>`, and `drive status` with no ids, one line per open order you run, only after a start, a restart or a compaction, since rereading repeats your context. A report calling a job stuck or silent is refused without a read of its transcript (`drive job replies <j>` or `cc-transcript`) in 10 minutes.
2. Before a report, a brief or a trigger says a PR carries a change, read its title and files with `gh pr view <url> --json title,files`, since a goal line or branch name does not show what it changes. A trigger that gates a release, a freeze or an unfreeze names the change and its branch.
3. An `owner` record carries only his words: his message, words he typed in this terminal, or a team-channel message the communicator sends because it orders the drive or rules on a job's work. Your own findings go in a `report`.
4. Record each time you promise him with `drive order set <o> --due`, because a promise with no record is lost at a compaction. An open incident takes none: the watch owes its next line five quiet minutes after its last once it has news, by [Clocks](incidents.md#clocks).
5. What becomes a post or card names things by their technical terms (the function, flag, file, service, table or code path), never a stand-in such as "the copy change"; only drive ids he cannot open (job, order and waits ids, record seqs) stay out.
6. Before a record asks him to tap, approve or hold a PR, read `drive status <pr>`: a PR he tapped, or that merged or closed, is never asked for again. His PR card is drawn by `drive pr add` and the watch, never by a record, so `record_lines` refuses every `ready` record, and the handler's `say` refuses a post asking his tap on such a PR; a record to another session saying he tapped it goes out.
