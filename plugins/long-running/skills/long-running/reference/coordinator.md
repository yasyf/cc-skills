# The coordinator

| Input | Do, in this turn |
|---|---|
| An order, in an `owner` record | `drive order add "<his words, verbatim>" --from slack:<channel>/<ts> --thread <ts> --parts "<part>"...` for each order in it, one part for each deliverable his words name (each stack, each design, each question); an order of one deliverable needs no `--parts`. Then decide for each part whether it carries a tricky design, by [Design first](design.md): such a part's first job is a design lane, and its builder may build the recommended options while his questions are open but puts up no PR until he has answered them. Give every part a background job now, in the order he stated them, each job naming the parts it delivers with `--part`. A part that asks for cards or a summary is delivered by those cards: its job reads and writes them, and no job builds a fix for one before his pick on its card, by [Scope](scope.md). Every order goes to a job: a past-due one, a one-line one and a session close too. A part found later is `drive order set <o> --parts` with the whole list. An order to land a change that a design card of his still open is on lands nothing: in this turn a `report` to his order's thread redraws that card, and the change lands on its `FREE` row once he answers, by [Design first](design.md). Set `--due` for when he hears next. |
| A ruling, in an `owner` record: any message of his that says how the drive, its two sessions, cards or messages should work, a correction included | A ruling on arrival, in the same turn and unasked. `answer_add` it as the communicator's row says, unless the record's `ruling` attr names the answer it already added. When it changes how drives work, start a builder on yasyf/cc-skills that rewrites the section of this skill it touches, merged and installed; its `DONE` becomes a `report` for his thread listing, in plain words, each rule now in the installed skill. A reader job started for it finds every live job the ruling touches; send each his words verbatim, with their time and what they mean for that job, with `SendMessage` when it returns, never the answer id alone. |
| A question of his, in an `owner` record, such as one a card's `ask` record does not answer | A `report` of what you have now inside 5 to 10 seconds, then the read in a background job when it takes more than one call, its result the second `report`. When a card of his he has not answered blocks the work he asks about, the `report` names each such card by its permalink and when it was posted, so the handler redraws each with its buttons in his thread: never "parked behind a question card" without the card, and never a plan to go ahead on its recommended option. His words, Nov 10 2:53 AM: "Did I answer those question cards they were blocked on or did you just skip them? You should surface them to me if I ask and they are blocking, not skip them". |
| An `answer` record | `answer_add` with the card's question as title and, as body, the option he tapped or his own words verbatim, never a paraphrase, a bot's summary, or the closest option standing in for words he wrote. A record with `maps=none` settles nothing: resume no job on it until he picks. A question that follows up his words on a card goes out as an `ask` with `--attr waits=<that card's waits id>`, which redraws that card in place with the new question, never as a second card, which leaves the first one listed as waiting on him. An answer to a design lane's question goes, verbatim, into the brief or handoff of that part's builder, by [Design first](design.md), and the design job is `done` once he has answered every question of that design; work built ahead of his answers starts its PR on the `FREE` row, never on the answer itself. Otherwise resume its job, or for a card from before the split the job that asked it: `SendMessage` to its agent with `drive job set <j> --state running`, or `drive job resume` for a remote lane. A record with `undone` takes that answer back: an answer he undid within ten minutes of his tap was never given, whatever started on it. `answer_add` the card's question again with the body `Undone: <label>. Never given; the card waits for his answer.`, and `answer_supersede` the earlier answer by it. Tell the job resumed on it, and a builder its words went into, with `SendMessage` (`drive job resume` for a remote lane): his label, the time he undid it, and that it was never given, so it stops what it started on it, keeps nothing built on it, and waits for his answer. Then `drive job set <j> --state waiting --card <the card's permalink>`, adding `--waits <the card's waits id>` for a job that built ahead of a design card, so the watch holds the card open again. His next tap is an ordinary `answer` record, whose answer supersedes the undone one. |
| A `refused` record | Read the refusal it quotes. An `ask` that carried a job's `ASK` goes back to the job its `job` attr names, with the refusal verbatim: `SendMessage` to its agent with `drive job set <j> --state running`, or `drive job resume <j> "<the refusal>"` for a remote lane. Its `ASK` comes back in shape and its row posts the new `ask`. A design fork's `ask`, which names no file, you rewrite into the shape the refusal asks for and post again in this turn. |
| A `defect` record | A `Drive tooling:` order of its own and a builder on that tool's repository, by [Tooling](../SKILL.md#tooling), never a job of the order whose work it blocked. |
| A message he typed in this terminal | An `owner` record of his words to `communicator@<drive>`, which acks it, marks him present and sends it back as his message. |
| A job finished, or a `JOB …` event | Its result is one line: do the matching row below. The job kept the rest, which `drive job report <j>` prints, and you read it only where a row reads past that line; a `report` for a handler names `drive job report <j>` in its body instead of copying the facts. |
| A watch event | One line each, by its row below. `TAP`, `DUE`, `JOB`, `LANE`, `EVENT catch-up` and `EVENT unheard` lines come at once; every other line is held and comes with the next of those, or 5 minutes after the watch last spoke, so CI and PR moves arrive together. Read only the rows a line names, `drive status <order, job or PR>`; the whole `drive status` is for after a start, restart or compaction, since rereading it on Fable for every event repeats what is already in context. |
| An incident: his order with incident priority, an alert he sent in an `owner` record, or an alert in a held channel the drive's own release or change caused | [reference/incidents.md](incidents.md), before anything else. After a release or a new build, in that turn the first check alone: how far the default branch is past the live build's commit, and whether its newest build works on a test machine, which makes releasing it the fix. Otherwise, or when that build fails too, a fix job whose first change stops users being blocked, a rollback job and a cause job that gates neither. He hears a line at least every 5 minutes until live. Any other alert gets nothing: no record, no job. |
| A record from another session | Answer it on cci as its kind asks. A PR or bug it hands the drive is neither a port nor his order unless an order of his names it, by [Scope](scope.md): a `handoff` record sends it back to that lane, with no job and no card. |

| Job result | Do |
|---|---|
| `DONE <proof>` | Once its last job is done, `drive order set <o> --proof <proof>`. The result goes into the next summary of his thread, or his catch-up when he is away: what changed for him, what it served (his order's words, or "port of #N landed" with the old PR linked), and the proof link; a drive tooling order's line says which tool now works and what it unblocks, at his DM root and never in a feature thread, in a `report` carrying `--attr close=<o>` (several orders space-separated), its body naming `drive job report <j>` for the facts. The handler that posts it closes each order with `drive order set <o> --state done --record <the report's seq>` once its line is in the thread, and drive refuses any order that record's `close` attr does not name, so only `close` ever closes an order: a report about an order that is not finished carries no `close` for it. Done and dropped also refuse while the order owes a promised line not posted there, so no order closes before he hears. A remote lane also gets `drive job end <j>`, run in the background, which frees its Sprite. |
| `READY <pr url>...` | A remote lane also gets `drive job end <j>`, run in the background. Read its kept return with `drive job report <j>`: its `SERVES:` line says what each PR is, by [Scope](scope.md). **A port**: read it against #N's diff with `gh pr diff`; one that does only what #N does gets `drive pr set <url> --port-of <N>`, which sets the `pure port` exemption, and a handoff builder lands it, with no card; anything past #N makes it his order's or neither. **His order**: read each PR body's `His words, step by step` list against his words in the order: every step he named sits beside the code that does it. A list that is missing, empty or says none is not his order: no card, whatever the rest of the PR says, and `drive card` refuses one. A step missing or unmatched goes back to the job, with no card. Then read its `DESIGN:` line, which names each design decision the PR makes beside the answer or card of his that settled it, or says none: a decision he never answered draws no card, and goes back to the job to return as an `ASK`, a design question by [Design first](design.md). A PR that carries a design card of his still open gets `drive pr set <url> --waits <card>` for each card and no card of its own, by [Design first](design.md). Then `drive pr set <url> --tap --serves <o> --design asked:<his answer id or card permalink>` for each URL, which refuses while a card or job it waits on holds, or `--design none` when the PR makes no design decision, which prints his words back so you read what they asked for and refuses, in `drive card`'s own words, a body his card would be refused for, and one `ready` record with every URL, bottom first, the order's thread, and his order's words quoted in its text. **Neither**: no card and no more work; another lane's PR goes back to that lane in a `handoff` record, and `drive pr set <url> --no-tap --why "<reason>"` withdraws any card already drawn for it, in place, so his card reads Withdrawn and the reason with no buttons and a late tap records nothing. **Drive tooling**: no PR card. A `Forge-AI/*` PR an exempt class covers gets `drive pr set <url> --exempt <class>` and a handoff builder lands it; any other comes back from its builder as an `ASK` whose question opens `Drive tooling:`, drawn at his DM root, and his pick to land it becomes `drive pr set <url> --tapped Approve --tap-source <card permalink>` and a handoff builder; a `yasyf/*` one its builder lands itself and returns `DONE`. A PR added to the stack later comes back as its own `READY` and gets its own card. |
| `WAITING <what>` | `drive job set <j> --state waiting --said "<what>"`, and for a remote lane `drive job end <j>` in the background. Nothing to him. Its open PR's next event gets a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page); a wait on anything else, such as a release run, also gets `drive order set <o> --due` for when to look. |
| `ASK <path>` | The job kept its block with `drive job ask`, which refuses one out of shape or one a recorded answer of his may settle, names each fix and sets the job waiting. A remote lane's `JOB … ASK` line gets `drive job ask <j>` here, which reads the lane's report; its refusal goes back with `drive job resume <j> "<the refusal>"`, and nothing goes to him. Then one `ask` record with the question as its text, `--attr ask=<the kept path>`, `--attr job=<j>` and the order's thread, never the block. Resume the job when his `answer` record arrives, or with the refusal when a `refused` record does. His rulings, Nov 9 6:22 PM: "you need to give me the results of the investigation etc before sahowing this to me", which the summary's first line, what was found, carries on the card; and 9:33 PM: "What the fuck is this insane shit you're sending me your messages need to be way shorter rewrite this for a human", so the findings stay in the file and reach him only when he taps Explain more. A design question rests on the design, not a measurement: its summary is the design in a line, and its findings are the design in plain words, what his words already settle, and what each option changes and costs. |
| `DESIGN <part>`, from a design lane | By [Design first](design.md). Read the design with `drive job report <j>`: each `Fork:` in it becomes one `ask` record, `--attr job=<j>`, its question and options verbatim as its text and its `Design:` and `Settled:` lines as its body, which reach him only when he taps Explain more, so each fork is its own short question card and nothing else; then `drive job set <j> --state waiting --said "<the questions>"`. The part's builder starts now, the design as its brief's `Plan:`: with no fork it builds and returns `READY`; with forks it builds each recommended option and returns `BUILT`, its `Decisions that bind` naming each open card and the option it builds. |
| `BUILT <branch>, waits on <cards>` | `drive job set <j> --state built --branch <branch> --waits <card>`, one `--waits` for each card's waits id or Slack link, and for a remote lane `drive job end <j>` in the background. No PR, no card and nothing to him: his status tab and the dashboard show it built, with the branch and each card, and `drive pr add` refuses a new PR for the job while one of them is open. The `FREE` row starts its PR. |
| `BLOCKED <what>` | Decide. A broken tool gets a builder job on that tool's repo now, and the blocked work moves to a route that works. Never hand a broken tool to him. `BLOCKED no order of his names this` is a PR that is drive tooling, which moves to an order of its own and lands as the `READY` row says, or neither, by [Scope](scope.md): no PR card, and it goes back to its lane or becomes a question card. |

| Event | Do |
|---|---|
| `PR … checks failed`, `conflicts with its base`, `changes requested` | A fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) carrying the event line. A `new head`, `checks passed` or `approved` alone is the job's own push or CI moving: nothing, unless the PR is now green and waits only on his tap, which takes the `READY` row for it. |
| `PR … merged` | A fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) to install and prove it live, or mark the order done when nothing is left to install. |
| `PR … closed` | If he closed it, the order's work on it ends. Otherwise a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) to reopen or replace it. |
| `TAP Approve …` | A fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page): land it. A stack card's Approve arrives as one `TAP Approve` per PR, bottom first; one handoff carries all of them. A PR that still waits on a card or job lands on its `FREE` row instead. |
| `TAP Hold …` | Nothing lands: start no handoff for that PR until he says otherwise. |
| `DUE o<n> "…" was due <time>: <why>` | The order owes him a line: the watch read its thread in Slack and found no drive post since the time was promised. It says so again every 10 minutes, once while he is away, until a post is there or his own words withdraw the time. Read the order's job transcripts first, set the new time with `--due`, then a `report` for that thread: what is late, why, and the new time. The new time is owed from that report's line, and the old one keeps firing until the line lands. Nothing but his own message ends a time that has no line: `drive order set <o> --withdrawn <its permalink>`. A `why` of no thread gets `drive order set <o> --thread <ts>`. While he is away it waits for his catch-up instead, except an incident's, which goes out at once by [reference/incidents.md](incidents.md). Never call a job silent, stuck or done without reading its transcript. |
| `TICK o<n> …` | The watch ticks every 30 minutes, never while he is away, and lists only orders with news: the order or one of its jobs changed since its last line, or its promised time falls before the next tick. One `report` per thread, grouped by feature, covering every listed order in that thread (his main thread for orders with none): what is done since the last line and what is next, or that the promised time holds. Then `drive order set <o> --lined` for each, with `--due` when the report names when he hears next: a time `drive order set --due` sets is owed from the next line in the order's thread, the one that tells him the time. An order with nothing new gets no line. |
| `EVENT catch-up thread=… orders=…` | He is back. One `report` for that thread covering those orders, grouped by feature, with what waits on him last, each with its card's permalink so the line draws the card under it, then `--lined` each. |
| `EVENT refused …` | cc-slack would not draw that PR's card, for the reason the line gives. The card is drawn from the PR, so a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) fixes the title or body, then send its `ready` again. A refusal saying no order of his names the PR is fixed by no rewrite, and never by writing his words for another change into its body: it is a port, which lands with no card, drive tooling, which moves to an order of its own and lands as the `READY` row says, or neither, which goes back by [Scope](scope.md). One saying it waits on a card or job is fixed by no rewrite either: nothing until the `FREE` row. |
| `EVENT unheard …` | He answered a card no session was listening to; the line carries the question, his pick and its link. Treat it as his `answer` to that card. |
| `ORPHAN o<n> "<part>": <why>` | A part of an open order has no live job and no open card: no job ever took it, its job finished with it left over, its agent or lane is gone, its card closed, a waiting job has nothing that can wake it, or a reader's, design or codex job's answer is neither posted to him nor on a question card he has not answered. A `running` job stuck this way is said whatever else carries the part, a posted answer or another job included: its agent or lane finished with no result recorded, or its lane ended and was not resumed within 30 minutes, or GitHub holds an open PR on its branch, or on the branch its lane's terminal is on, that the drive does not track. The why then names the job, its lane's state, and each PR it has open with its checks and review. In this turn, start a job for it, have a running job that already does that work carry it, resume or restart its job, or have its card drawn; an answer not yet posted goes to the communicator as a `report` read from its job's transcript. A finished job's result takes its row in the results table, read with `drive job report <j>` or `drive job replies <j>`, and a PR the drive does not track gets `drive pr add <url> --job <j>`. A part already delivered or one he withdrew leaves the order with `drive order set <o> --parts` or the order closes with `--state done` or `dropped`. Never leave one standing: the watch says it again every 30 minutes and at its order's `DUE`, until the job's state changes or the drive tracks the PR. |
| `PR … still red for <n> minutes`, `green and approved with no card drawn for <n> minutes` | A PR that has not moved for 30 minutes, said once, kept in the drive across watch restarts, and again only when something about it changes: its head, checks, review, tap, its job's state, or what it waits on. A red one gets a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page); a green one, said only of a PR that waits on his tap and is not held, takes the `READY` row above, so a port or a PR that is neither gets no card. A PR that waits on something first, such as his open design card or a job's real run, gets `drive pr set <url> --waits <the card's Slack link or waits id, or the job>`, once for each, and `--no-waits` drops them all. Adding one withdraws any Approve card drawn for it; while one holds no card is owed, so no green line comes, and a red line ends `waiting on …`. The `FREE` row says when the last ends. `drive view` and the dashboard list it the whole time. |
| `FREE <j> <o>: … nothing else holds it, built on <branch>`, `FREE <pr> <j> <o>: …` | The last card or job a job or PR waits on is answered or done, said once; the watch drops each wait as it ends. A built job gets a fresh builder with a handoff (the `jobs` page) carrying his answers verbatim: it rebuilds the branch where his pick differs from the recommended option it built, opens the PR and returns `READY`. A PR gets the same handoff where his pick differs from what it built; otherwise one that waits on his tap takes the `READY` row now, and one he approved lands. A job that waited with nothing built resumes. |
| `JOB <j> <o> STOPPED …` | A remote lane ended its turn without a report. Read its screen (the `jobs` page), then resume it with what to do next. |
| `LANE <j> <o> died on Sprite …` | A remote lane's worker died, and its session with it. In this turn, `drive job resume <j> -` with its next step on stdin in the background relaunches it with a handoff; a lane that waits on his answer is resumed with that answer when it comes. |
| `WATCH compacted the communicator …` | Nothing: the watch typed `/compact` into the communicator's Orca terminal at a quiet moment. |
| `WATCH … failed` | GitHub or Orca did not answer; the watch keeps trying and says so again in ten minutes. Nothing to him. |
| `WATCH status page stale since …` | Slack refused the status page's redraw, so his canvas still shows that time's state; the line names Slack's reason. The watch retries each change and says it once, kept in the drive across watch restarts, then `WATCH status page redrawn …` once when Slack takes a redraw again. Nothing to him; one that persists gets a builder on cc-slack. |
| `WATCH status page redrawn on a new tab: <url>` | The drive's status tab moved: Slack called the old one full, or it was gone, so the watch drew the page on a fresh tab of the same name in his DM. The next line to him that says where things stand links the new tab. |

While he is away, send only `ready` and `ask` records and the incident lines
[reference/incidents.md](incidents.md) calls for; everything else waits for his catch-up.
