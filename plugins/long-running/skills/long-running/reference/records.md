# Between the two sessions

Post with the cci `post` tool or `cci post`, naming the writer with `--lane communicator` or
`--lane root`, and address the other as `root@<drive>` or `communicator@<drive>`: a bare lane no
live session reads here can land on another drive. A handler the communicator starts writes to
`communicator@<drive>` as `--lane handler`, and a builder it starts as `--lane builder`, passed as
`lane` on the `post` tool too: an agent shares its session's lane, the tool's default, and cci
refuses a record whose every `--to` is the lane posting it, since a session reads a record from its
own lane as one it wrote. Every other record they post keeps `--lane communicator`. On Nov 10 a
handler's note and a builder's report to the communicator were both refused as the communicator's
own. Each record names the thread it is about with
`--attr thread=<ts>`. A record's text is one line of at most 120 characters, about one terminal
line, its ids and paths in place of prose, since the channel puts that line whole into the other
session's context; the rest goes in its body, which only the handler or job that acts on it reads
with `cci show <seq>`. In a drive session the `post` tool cuts a longer text itself: the line
becomes its first line, cut at a word and ending in `…`, and the whole text moves to the top of the
body; a record that attaches a file as its body is refused instead, so the rest goes in that file.
His words stay whole: `owner`, `answer` and `incident` records are never cut. His pick, Nov 10
2:08 AM: "Job reports, alerts, role-to-role notes and CI events arrive as one line each, CI events
batched per 5 minutes".

| Record | From | To | Carries | The receiver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `owner` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | his words verbatim, his message's permalink as `--url`, his thread, and `--attr ruling=<answer id>` on a ruling | makes them orders, or applies a ruling to the live work by its table; the communicator starts the ruling's skill rewrite itself |
| `answer` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | `--re` the `ask` whose card he answered, the seq its `waits` id ends in, `--attr job=<j>`, and his pick or his own words; the ask itself closed when its card went up, so only this record says he answered | records his answer and resumes that job |
| `defect` | `root`, its jobs, or another session | `communicator@<drive>` | a tool defect it hit, any refusal verbatim; a handler or builder of the communicator's returns it as a `DEFECT` line instead, which the communicator starts the builder from, so it posts no `defect` record that would start a second one | starts a drive tooling builder on that tool's repository |
| `handoff` | the watch, as `root` | `communicator@<drive>` | one watch line about a drive tooling order that needs a step, such as a red check, his Approve tap, a `FREE` or an `ORPHAN` | starts a builder briefed with the record, which does that step; the coordinator never hears these lines |
| `report` | `root`, or a handler or builder of the communicator's as `handler` or `builder` | `communicator@<drive>` | the facts of one post, opening `POST` (a handler posts it) or `FYI` (context, nothing posted): the thread or alert link it goes in, or none for his catch-up, which carries `--attr catch_up=true` and goes at his DM root, where `say` opens it with the title `*Catch-up since <the time he was last heard>*`, one plain line per feature saying what changed for him, each thing in it named by what it is rather than by a step or job name he has not used himself, a function, file or other technical thing by its proper term ("the button a person must press before a customer cluster's changes go out", never "the roll press"), its link and proof, each thing it names that he could open, a PR, fix, release, build, alert or thread, with its URL beside it so the post links it where it is named, and for each item that waits on him its card's permalink or `waits` id; never a drive id, since the post holds at most 120 words, and its body names `drive job report <j>` for each job whose kept return holds the facts, never copies them. `--attr close=<o …>` names each order the post finishes and is the only order attr a report carries: an order it is only about is named in no attr, since the handler closes what `close` names and drive refuses it any other. `--attr part=<name>` names the part of its order the post delivers, and a report carrying a job's answer names that job with `--attr job=<j>`, which delivers each part of the order the job delivers, an incident's first check or cause included; a progress line names neither | a `POST` gets a handler that writes and posts it with `say(order=<o>, record=<its seq>)`, which records it on the order and delivers the parts its `part` or `job` attr names, so a progress line keeps his promised time and delivers no part, then closes each order `close` names with `drive order set <o> --state done --record <seq>`; an `FYI` is kept as context |
| `ready` | `root` | `communicator@<drive>` | a stack's PR URLs, bottom first, and in its text the order it carries out, quoted for the line of at most 60 words above his card: his words, or a drive tooling order's "Drive tooling: …" words with no thread; never a port and never a PR that is neither | draws the stack's PR card |
| `ask` | `root` | `communicator@<drive>` | the question as its text, `--attr ask=<the path drive job ask printed>`, a file for this question alone that the job's next question never overwrites, since a job keeps every question it asks open until his answer to its own card, and `--attr job=<j>`, never the block itself, a design fork's included; `--attr waits=<an open card's waits id>` when it redraws that card, and `--attr reworking=<what is being redone, and when it is back>` when it marks that card as being redone | draws one question card, the one `drive job card <file> --waits <id>` prints from the file's question, summary, Show me and options, its `waits` id `<drive>:<j>-<the record's seq>`, given the order's thread, which cc-slack answers with the card at his DM root and its permalink in that thread under the card's question, where Slack draws the card, closes the ask with a `posted` record, and posts nothing else: the findings stay in the file for Explain more. One with `waits` redraws that card in one call under that id, which edits it in place and posts nothing, every older copy losing its buttons; with `reworking` too, the card keeps its words and buttons and gains one line atop its body, drawn by `drive job card --reworking`, saying the question is being redone and why |
| `posted` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | `--resolves` the `ask` whose card is up, the card's permalink as `--url` and `--attr waits=<its waits id>` | nothing: `root` does not hear `posted`, so it wakes no one, and it is not his answer; the card stays his open question in `drive view` and the watch until his `answer` arrives |
| `refused` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | `--resolves` the `ask` it refuses and why, such as the card cc-slack or the length hook refused, quoted | resumes that job to fix its `ASK` and keep it again with `drive job ask`, which sends a new `ask` |
