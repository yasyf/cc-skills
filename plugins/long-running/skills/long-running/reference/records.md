# Between the two sessions

- A session posts its own `owner`, `answer` or `defect` with the drive's `record` tool, which picks its lane and addresses the other session, or the lane its `to` names, refusing a lane no session of the drive reads. An `answer` naming `waits` goes to the coordinator session hearing that job's order. Every other record, and any from an agent, goes through cci's `post` tool, whose `record_lines` hook checks it and `cci post` in Bash skips.
- On cci, name the writer with `--lane communicator` or `--lane root`, since cci's default is never `root`, and address `root@<drive>` or `communicator@<drive>`, since a bare lane no session here reads can land on another drive.
- A sub-coordinator, the coordinator session for one project, writes as `--lane <lane>` and is addressed at `<lane>@<drive>`, `<lane>` being the project's name lowercased with each run of other characters a dash (`Iris` is `iris`, `Sand SQL` is `sand-sql`). Its block names the lane, it reads at cursor `coordinator-<lane>`, and its project's watch lines reach it there.
- The drive routes an `owner`, `answer`, `posted` or `refused` record from the communicator's session, its handlers included, to the coordinator session hearing the order it serves: by its `job` or `waits` attr, else by its `thread`, whatever `to` it names. So name `root@<drive>` and let it route; a record naming neither keeps its `to`, and a `defect` is never routed.
- An agent of the communicator's posting to `communicator@<drive>` passes its own lane, such as `--lane handler` (`lane` on the `post` tool), since it shares its session's lane and cci refuses a record whose every `--to` is the lane posting it.
- A record about a thread names it with `--attr thread=<ts>`. A record's text is one line of at most 120 characters, ids and paths in place of prose, because the channel puts it whole into the other session's context; the rest goes in the body, read with `cci show <seq>`. No record carries a `max_words` attr: nothing reads it, his limits of 60 words a line and 120 a summary hold for every post, and `record_lines` refuses one. A `report`'s attrs keep the names the drive reads: `catch_up`, `close`, `part`, `job`, `thread` and `waits`. The drive reads `catchup`, `catch-up` or `closes` as missing, so `record_lines` refuses a key a separator, a capital or one letter from one of those and names the attr to use.
- `record_lines` refuses a `report`, `ready` or `ask` that breaks its row below, naming the fix, and a reader's or designer's record to `owner`. It cuts a longer text at a word, ending `…`, with the whole text atop the body, or alone when an attached file holds the rest, as the drive's `record` tool does for a `defect`. It never cuts an `owner`, `answer` or `incident`, so his words stay whole.
- Only an open record, such as an `ask`, takes `--resolves`; answer an `owner` or `report`, which opens nothing, with `--re <seq>`.

| Record | From | To | Carries | The receiver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `owner` | communicator, or a coordinator for words he typed in its terminal | `root@<drive>`, which the drive routes as below; `communicator@<drive>` from a coordinator | his words verbatim; his message's permalink as `--url`; his thread; `--attr ruling=<answer id>` on a ruling | makes his words orders, or applies a ruling to the live work by its table, while the communicator starts its skill rewrite; the communicator takes one from the coordinator as his message |
| `answer` | communicator | `root@<drive>`, which the drive routes as below | `--re` the `ask` whose card he answered, the seq its `waits` id ends in, and `--attr job=<j>`, which the `record` tool fills from `waits`; his pick or his own words; `undone` on an Undo tap. The ask closed when its card went up, so only this record says he answered. | records his answer and resumes that job |
| `defect` | a coordinator, its jobs, or another session | `communicator@<drive>` | a tool defect it hit, any refusal verbatim. A handler returns a `DEFECT` line instead, so only one fix starts; `record_lines` refuses its `defect`. | starts a drive tooling reader on that tool's repository, and its builder once the reader's research is kept |
| `handoff` | the watch, as lane `watch` | `communicator@<drive>` | one watch line about a drive tooling order that needs a step, such as a red check, his Approve tap, a `FREE` or an `ORPHAN` | starts a builder briefed with the record to do that step; the coordinator never hears these lines, since drive tooling is the communicator's |
| `report` | a coordinator, or the communicator's agents under their own lane | `communicator@<drive>` | the facts of one post, opening `POST` for a handler to post or `FYI` for context, and the thread or alert link it goes in. A report names one of its order's threads, the only place a post keeps his promised time, and one with `close` or `part` naming another is refused; `drive order set <o> --thread <ts>` adds one. His catch-up carries `--attr catch_up=true` and no thread, for his DM root, and `say` titles it `*Catch-up since <when he was last heard>*`. The facts give one plain line per feature saying what changed for him, each thing named by its proper technical term, never a step or job name he has not used ("the roll press") or a vague stand-in. Each PR, fix, release, build, alert or thread he could open carries its URL, so the post links it where named, and each item waiting on him its card's permalink or `waits` id. A `POST` is at most 60 words, 120 with two bold feature lines; it names no job, order or `waits` id or record seq, which he cannot open; the body names `drive job report <j>` for each job whose kept return holds the facts, never copying them. `--attr close=<o …>` names each order the post finishes, its only order attr, since the handler closes what `close` names and drive refuses any other. `--attr part=<name>` names the part the post delivers, a report carrying a job's answer included, an incident's first check or cause among them, and `--attr job=<j>` the job whose answer it carries, which delivers nothing; a progress line names no part and closes nothing. | a `POST` gets a handler that writes and posts it with `say(order=<o>, record=<its seq>)`, which records it on the order and delivers the part its `part` attr names, or each part of an order its `close` attr names, so a progress line keeps his promised time and delivers no part. It then closes each order `close` names with `drive order set <o> --state done --record <seq>`. An `FYI` is kept as context. |
| `ready` | a coordinator | `communicator@<drive>` | a stack's PR URLs, bottom first, and in its text the order it carries out, quoted for the line of at most 60 words above his card: `his order o<n>` and his words, or a drive tooling order's "Drive tooling: …" words with no thread, refused without either. It names only PRs serving one of those orders, never a port; `record_lines` refuses a PR the drive owes no tap on. | draws the stack's PR card |
| `ask` | a coordinator | `communicator@<drive>` | the question as its text; `--attr ask=<the path drive job ask printed>`, a file of this question alone, since each question stays open until his answer to its own card; `--attr job=<j>`, refused without; never the block itself, a design fork's included. A block asking again, in the same words, what an open question of the job asks needs nothing more, since `drive job ask` keeps it as that question; add `--attr waits=<an open card's waits id>` only to redraw that card with a question in new words, and `--attr reworking=<what is being redone, and when it is back>` to mark it as being redone. | draws the question card `drive job card <file> --waits <drive>:<j>-<the record's seq>` prints, under the waits id its question already holds when the job asked it before, so one question keeps one card. cc-slack posts it at his DM root with its permalink in the order's thread under the card's question, where Slack draws the card. The communicator then closes the ask with a `posted` record and posts nothing else, since the findings stay in the file for Explain more. With `waits`, one call redraws that card in place, posting nothing, every older copy losing its buttons; with `reworking` too, `drive job card --reworking` adds one line atop its body saying the question is being redone and why. `drive job card` refuses to redraw a card his words under it answered, because they are his answer. |
| `posted` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | `--resolves` the `ask` whose card is up; the card's permalink as `--url`; `--attr waits=<its waits id>` | nothing, since the coordinator's subscription leaves `posted` out. The card stays his open question in `drive view` and the watch until his `answer` arrives |
| `refused` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | `--resolves` the `ask` it refuses, and why, quoted, such as the card cc-slack or the length hook refused | resumes that job with the refusal to fix its `ASK` and keep it again with `drive job ask`, then posts its new `ask` |
