# Between the two sessions

Post with the cci `post` tool or `cci post`, naming the writer with `--lane communicator` or `--lane root`, and address the other as `root@<drive>` or `communicator@<drive>` so a bare lane no live session reads here cannot land on another drive. A handler or builder the communicator starts posts to `communicator@<drive>` with `--lane handler` or `--lane builder` (passed as `lane` on `post` too), and keeps `--lane communicator` on every other record: an agent shares its session's lane by default, and cci refuses a record whose every `--to` matches its writer's lane. Name the thread with `--attr thread=<ts>`. Keep a record's text to one line of at most 120 characters with ids and paths in place of prose, since the channel puts that line whole into the other session's context; put the rest in the body, which only the handler or job acting on it reads with `cci show <seq>`. For records other than `owner`, `answer` and `incident`, the `record_lines` hook cuts longer text to one line ending in `…` and moves the whole text to the body, or denies the call when a file is attached as the body.

| Record | From | To | Carries | The receiver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `owner` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | his words verbatim, his message's permalink as `--url`, his thread, and `--attr ruling=<answer id>` on a ruling | makes them orders, or applies a ruling to the live work by its table; the communicator starts the ruling's skill rewrite itself |
| `answer` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | `--re` the `ask` whose card he answered, the seq its `waits` id ends in, `--attr job=<j>`, and his pick or his own words | records his answer and resumes that job |
| `defect` | `root`, its jobs, or another session | `communicator@<drive>` | a tool defect it hit, any refusal verbatim; a communicator's handler or builder returns a `DEFECT` line instead so no second builder starts | starts a drive tooling builder on that tool's repository |
| `handoff` | the watch, as `root` | `communicator@<drive>` | one watch line about a drive tooling order that needs a step, such as a red check, his Approve tap, a `FREE` or an `ORPHAN` | starts a builder briefed with the record to do that step |
| `report` | `root`, or a communicator's handler or builder as `handler` or `builder` | `communicator@<drive>` | the facts of one post, opening `POST` (a handler posts it) or `FYI` (context): the thread or alert link, or none for a DM root catch-up opening `*Catch-up since <time>*`; one plain line per feature saying what changed for him, naming each thing by what it is rather than an internal name, with its URL beside every PR, fix, release, build, alert or thread he could open, and each waiting item's card permalink or `waits` id; no paths or ids in the post, and `drive job report <j>` in the body rather than copied facts. Pass `--attr close=<o …>` only for orders the post finishes, and `--attr part=<name>` only for a part it delivers | for `POST`, starts a handler that writes and posts it, runs `drive order posted <o> <link>` (with `--part <name>` only from a `part` attr), and closes each `close` order with `drive order set <o> --state done --record <seq>` (`drive order set` refuses without a matching `close` attr); keeps `FYI` as context |
| `ready` | `root` | `communicator@<drive>` | a stack's PR URLs, bottom first, and the order it carries out quoted for the line of at most 60 words above his card: his words, or a drive tooling order's "Drive tooling: …" words with no thread, never a port or any other PR | draws the stack's PR card |
| `ask` | `root` | `communicator@<drive>` | the question as text, `--attr ask=<the path drive job ask printed>` (a file for this question alone so the job's next question does not overwrite it while open), and `--attr job=<j>`, never the block itself; `--attr waits=<an open card's waits id>` to redraw that card in place, plus `--attr reworking=<what is being redone, and when it is back>` to add a `Being reworked` fact while keeping its words and buttons | draws the card `drive job card <file> --waits <id>` prints under `waits` id `<drive>:<j>-<the record's seq>` in the order's thread (or edits `<id>` in place when `waits` is set, stripping older copies' buttons), and closes the ask with `posted` |
| `posted` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | `--resolves` the `ask` whose card is up, the card's permalink as `--url` and `--attr waits=<its waits id>` | nothing: `root` does not hear `posted`, and the card stays open in `drive view` and the watch until `answer` arrives |
| `refused` | communicator | `root@<drive>` | `--resolves` the `ask` it refuses and why, quoted | resumes that job to fix its `ASK` and run `drive job ask` again |
