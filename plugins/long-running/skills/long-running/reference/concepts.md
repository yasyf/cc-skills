# Concepts

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| Order | One thing he asked for: his words verbatim, a state, an optional due time, its parts, its jobs, and its proof. Each part is one deliverable his words name, and holds a live job or an unanswered card until delivered; its jobs are those it started or that carry one of its parts. The one exception is a drive tooling fix: its words open `Drive tooling:`, its lines go at his DM root (`drive order add` refuses `--thread` or a coordinator call, and `drive pr set` and `drive job set` refuse mixing tooling and feature orders). `drive order …` |
| Job | One background agent owning one deliverable, started by one order (`drive job add` refuses a coordinator job on a `Drive tooling:` order). When a later order asks for work a running job already owns, `drive job set <j> --carries <o> [--part "<part>"]` attaches that part until the job finishes. Kinds: `long-running:builder` (one step of a change through installed and proven, or in state `built` on a pushed branch with no PR while `--waits` holds on open design cards), `long-running:reader` (one read-only question on Opus, or `model: "sonnet"` for a one-fact lookup), `long-running:designer` (initial read-only design and questions on Fable, by [Design first](design.md)), and `codex:codex-wrapper` (review, diagnosis, incidents); the `agent_brief` hook denies wrong model or brief shapes. The communicator's per-message `long-running:handler`s are not jobs. A monorepo builder started while this Mac is over its load limit runs as a remote lane on a Sprite. A job returns one line and stores the rest with `drive job report`. `drive job …` |
| Event | A one-line notice from `drive watch`: `PR …` state changes (without title), `PR … still red` or `… with no card drawn` after 30 minutes and again only when its state or `--waits` changes (no card is owed while a wait holds), `FREE …` once all `--waits` and asked cards clear, `TAP …` on his PR cards, `JOB …` remote lane reports, `LANE … died` once when a remote worker dies, `DUE …` every 10 minutes from a promised time until a drive post lands in the order's thread, `ORPHAN …` immediately when an open part has no carrier, a running job ends without a recorded result, or an untracked PR opens, `TICK …` every 30 minutes while he is here for orders with news or a due time close, `ANSWER …` every 10 minutes while an answer of his leaves a card or unmerged PR unmarked, and `EVENT catch-up …` when he returns (naming his DM and last-heard time for a DM-root catch-up). `TAP`, `DUE`, `ANSWER`, `JOB`, `LANE`, catch-up, and unheard lines arrive immediately; the rest batch at most every 5 minutes. |
| Record | A one-line cci record between the two sessions with detail in its body, by [Between the two sessions](records.md). |
| Message | An ack or summary line in his thread, a question card at his DM root, or a PR card (one per stack of up to four PRs, checked by `drive pr set --tap` and every card draw) that cc-slack draws from the PRs. [reference/messages.md](messages.md) |
| Status | Where the drive stands in `drive view`: what waits on him in priority order (open question cards blocking the most green approved PRs and built branches first, PRs ready for his tap, passed promised times), then each feature by state (blocked, running, built with its branch, waiting, merged, live, not started) with its blocking cards. `drive dashboard` serves this across seven live-updating screens—Your queue (numbered cards answered in place from this Mac or his tailnet via `cc-slack card-answer`, and typed notes routed via `cc-slack dm-relay`), Now, Status, Review (open PRs with diffs, threads, and change boxes), Chat (his asks, threads, and reply boxes), Work (jobs by state or quiet 45+ minutes), and History—plus a Cmd-K jump palette, an alerts bar, and browser notifications on secure contexts (tailnet HTTPS or loopback). `drive watch` also renders it to his DM canvas tab and writes open cards to `cards.json` every minute. |
