# Concepts

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| Order | One thing he asked for, in his words verbatim, because a paraphrase is not the spec. It holds a state, a kind, its parts, its jobs, its proof that it works where he uses it, its project and workstream, its short title, an optional due time, when a post promised him his next line (`drive order set <o> --due`), and its owner, the session that adds its jobs, starts its agents, checks its PRs against his words and posts its reports: `drive job add` and the Agent hook refuse the other sessions, naming the owner's session id to send a record to, and `say` and `drive order posted` refuse another session's report on it, naming the report to pass: on the communicator's own order, one its handler posts from the communicator's lane. Kind `answer` is one reader's research and answer; `build` puts an Opus reader's research before any builder or designer. A part is one deliverable his words name, such as each stack of a design or each question. Every part of an order neither an answer order nor limited to options is work he ordered: only a builder, codex or design job carries it, and only a builder or codex job returning `DONE` delivers it, never research, his card or a post. Every other part is words he reads, delivered by the drive's post whose report names it in its `part` attr, or by the post a finished job's `POSTED <link>` return names, which closes an answer order once every part is posted; keep a live job or an unanswered card on every part until it is delivered, so he never has to remind the drive. Its jobs are those it started and those carrying one of its parts. The one order not in his words is drive tooling, how the drive itself works: a tool it runs on (the drive CLI and its MCP tools, a hook, the watch, the dashboard, cards and posts, cc-slack check-post, the drive's CI plumbing), this skill, its agents and role rules, how its sessions reach each other and him, and the install of a released tool. Its words open `Drive tooling:` and the meta session owns it, which every drive runs beside the communicator and the coordinator. It keeps the thread his words ordering it came in, so his later words there find it, and else has none, its posts going at his DM root; it carries no part of a feature order, so a feature's thread keeps one subject. `drive order …`, the `order` tool. |
| Job | One background agent owning one deliverable, started by one order. When a later order asks for work a running job owns, that job carries it, `drive job set <j> --carries <o> [--part "<part>"]`, listed under both orders as that part's live job until done. `long-running:builder`: one step of one change, the code and its open PR, or one event on that PR through a handoff, up to installed and proven; while design cards of his are open, the recommended option on a pushed branch, in state `built` with `--branch` and a `--waits` on each card, and outside an incident's repair the `bash_guard` hook refuses the `ccx vcs ship` that would open its PR until he answers every card. `long-running:reader`: one question an order needs, read only, on Opus, `model: "sonnet"` only for a one-fact lookup. `long-running:designer`: the initial design of a tricky part and its questions for him, read only, on Fable, by [Design first](design.md). `codex:codex-wrapper`: review, diagnosis, incidents. The communicator's `long-running:handler`s, one per message of his, are not jobs, except one answering a read-only question, which `drive handler` records as its answer order's `reader` job so the watch holds the answer to him. Over this Mac's load limit (`drive place`), a monorepo builder whose brief names no step only this Mac runs starts on a Sprite with `drive job launch`. A job returns one line and keeps the rest with `drive job report <j> -`. [Jobs](jobs.md) lists the hooks that refuse other shapes. `drive job …`, the `job` tool. |
| Event | One line from a watch, the drive's own or a sub-coordinator's for its project, each a process of its own, a PR's without its title. `PR …` says a tracked PR changed, Trunk ejected it from the merge queue, the drive's lander queued, merged or failed to land it, or it sat 30 minutes red, green and approved with no card drawn, or green, approved, exempt and not landed; `TAP …`, he tapped Approve or Hold on a PR card, and `TAP Approve … not landed …`, a PR he approved has had no lander for 10 minutes, naming why; `FREE …`, the last card, job or PR a job or PR waits on is answered, done or merged; `WAITING-ON-HIM …`, a card of his started holding a job built on its recommended option, and the watch replied to him once, in plain words, in that card's own thread; `JOB …`, a remote lane reported or stopped; `LANE … died`, a remote lane's worker died, and the watch relaunched a running job's lane that pass; `STUCK …`, once per call, a local job's tool call has had no result for 5 minutes, held by a permission prompt nobody sees; `DUE …`, a promised time passed with no post in its order's thread, which an open incident never has, since the watch owes its next line five quiet minutes after its last and starts the handler that posts it; `DUE j<n> …`, once, a job's clock ran out while it still runs; `RECOVERED o<n> …`, an open incident is over, its fix live and any monitor of it OK, and the watch sent its step 8 report, which closes it; `TELL o<n> …`, once, an order's fix is proven live, or an answer order's answer is posted, and its reporter's thread has no note 10 minutes on; `CARD … settled in place`, every PR his open PR card carries merged or closed; `ANSWER …`, an answer of his leaves cards or PRs unmarked; `ORPHAN …`, a part of an open order has nothing carrying it, one no job took once its order sat still five minutes, or its job is stuck, gone, or has an untracked open PR; `TICK …`, every 30 minutes while he is here, the orders with news or a promised time close. The watch also says `EVENT refused …` and `WATCH …`. A `Drive tooling:` order's lines go to the meta session, a PR's move to the remote lane of the job waiting on it, and the rest over cci to the coordinator session hearing the order, the sub-coordinator bound for its project or else the top: `TAP`, `FREE`, `DUE`, `TELL`, `ORPHAN`, `ANSWER`, `JOB`, `LANE`, `STUCK` and `WATCH stopped` lines and a PR's line whose job is not running wake it at once, the rest at most every 5 minutes; a PR's own new head, checks passed, approval or `queued for merge` wakes it only when the PR then waits on its landing as an exempt class, and his `TAP Approve` of a `Forge-AI/monorepo` PR only when the PR is red or conflicts, or no lander has taken it for 10 minutes, since the lander queues it otherwise. `drive manual coordinator <kind>` prints when each repeats and what to do. |
| Record | A cci record on the drive between its sessions, one line with the rest in its body, by [Between the sessions](records.md). |
| Message | A post to him in Slack, in a shape from [the messages page](messages.md): mainly an ack or summary in his thread, a question card at his DM root, or a PR card that cc-slack draws from the PRs, one per stack whatever its size, the PR sections past the 50 blocks one Slack message holds drawn under its Explain more: a PR's native GitHub stack, the one `stack-enqueue` lands, read on every draw, a PR added to it after its card went up getting its own card, else the PRs its base branch chains it to, and for a PR in neither, the PRs asked with it of its repository that serve its order: one rule, `state.card_unit`, which the watch's `ready` record names them by too, so sibling PRs of one order ready together draw one card and his Approve covers each. |
| Answer | His reply to a card or a question; a ruling is a standing direction of his. `drive answer` records each verbatim in cc-notes and marks every open card and PR his words may touch as standing, settled, superseded or reopened, Jev reading each new answer against every open question card, so a card a later decision settles is settled or redrawn to ask only what it leaves open. His words under a card answer it, and a question they leave open goes on a new card. `drive job card` refuses to redraw a card his words already answered, and `drive job ask` keeps a question settled with no card only on his answer to that same question, asked in the same words on or under its card or recorded under it, never on an answer he gave another question. A ruling binds only what his words name, and his feedback on a post or card names the library, `cc-slack check-post` and drive tools that let it through, by [His words](../SKILL.md#his-words). |
| Status | One model of where the drive stands, `drive view`, drawn by the dashboard and the Slack status page. First what waits on him, by priority: his open question cards, those holding up the most green approved PRs and built branches first, each saying how many; then PRs ready for his tap; then promised times that passed. Then each feature by state, naming the open question cards blocking it and when each was posted, and a built feature its branch. `drive dashboard` serves four screens: Your queue, one list of his cards and PRs in the order the Slack page's tables list them, filtered to one project or workstream by a link, each card drawn whole with the features it blocks; Now, open orders by project and workstream; Work, running and waiting jobs by project and workstream, flagging any quiet 45 minutes or more; History, each day newest first. Cmd-K jumps to any question, change, feature or job; a top strip shows only live incidents, open bug reports and how many questions and PR taps wait on him, since he asked to see only what he can act on; a bug report counts until its reporter is told the fix is live, and its list says the stage each report reached: reported, being fixed, merged, live and not told, or told. A pick, Approve or Hold there goes through `cc-slack card-answer`, so the Slack card, asking session and PR tap all hear a Slack tap, and an Approve or Hold on a PR with no card is recorded as his tap from the dashboard, which its lander hears as a Slack tap; only this Mac or his own tailnet devices may answer. Words he types under a card reach his DM through `cc-slack dm-relay`, marked as typed on the dashboard, and the drive hears them as his DM. The page updates on each change to the drive's files and, in a secure context such as its https tailnet link or its loopback link on this Mac, notifies the browser of each new thing waiting on him. The watch also draws it as the drive's canvas tab in his DM and stores his open cards in `cards.json` every minute. |
