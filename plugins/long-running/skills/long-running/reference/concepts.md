# Concepts

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| Order | One thing he asked for: his words verbatim, a state, an optional due time, its parts, its jobs, its proof. A part is one deliverable his words name, such as each stack of a design or each question; every part has a live job or a card he has yet to answer, until it is delivered. Its jobs are those it started and those carrying one of its parts. `drive order …` |
| Job | One background agent owning one deliverable, started by one order. When a later order of his asks for work a running job already owns, that job carries the later order's part too, `drive job set <j> --carries <o> [--part "<part>"]`, listed under both orders and counted as that part's live job until the job is done: `long-running:builder` (one step of one change: the code and its open PR, or one event on that PR through a handoff, up to installed and proven), `long-running:reader` (one question an order needs, read only, on Sonnet), `long-running:designer` (the initial design of a part with a tricky design and its questions for him, read only, on Fable, by [Design first](design.md)), `codex:codex-wrapper` (review, diagnosis, incidents). The communicator's `long-running:handler`s, one per message of his, are not jobs. A monorepo builder started while this Mac is over its load limit is a remote lane on a Sprite instead. `drive job …` |
| Event | A line from `drive watch`: `PR …` state changes, `TAP …` on his PR cards, `JOB …` reports from remote lanes, `LANE … died` once when a remote lane's worker dies, `DUE …` from a promised time, every 10 minutes, until a drive post is in its order's thread, `ORPHAN …` the moment a part of an open order has nothing carrying it, `TICK …` every 30 minutes while he is here, naming the orders with news since their last line or a promised time close, `EVENT catch-up …` when he is back. |
| Record | A cci record on the drive between the two sessions, by [Between the two sessions](records.md). |
| Message | An ack or a summary line in his thread, a question card at his DM root, or a PR card, one per stack, that cc-slack draws from the PRs. Nothing else. [reference/messages.md](messages.md) |
| Status | One model of where the drive stands, `drive view`: what waits on him, highest priority first (his open question cards, PRs ready for his tap, promised times that passed), then each feature by state (blocked, running, waiting, merged, live, not started). `drive dashboard` serves it as seven screens, Your queue (each card drawn whole, the same card Slack draws, and answered in place: a pick or Approve/Hold goes through `cc-slack card-answer`, so the Slack card, the asking session and the PR's tap all hear it as a Slack tap; only this Mac or his own tailnet devices may answer), Now (what needs him, one sentence on where the drive stands, what is blocked and what is moving), Status (overall progress, each feature's PR bar and its own page with his words, jobs and PRs), Review (every open PR, waiting on him, not ready yet, or approved or held, each opening in place with its description and diff), Chat (every ask of his, with its Slack thread drawn on the page and any card in it answerable there), Work (jobs grouped as quiet 45 minutes or more, running, waiting and finished in the last day) and History (the last day), with a jump palette (Cmd-K) over every question, change, feature and job, an alerts bar for quiet jobs, passed promises and red PRs, and a browser notification for each new thing that waits on him wherever the page is a secure context (on this Mac at its loopback link), pushed to the page on each change to the drive's files, and `drive watch` draws it as the drive's canvas tab in his DM and stores his open cards in the drive's `cards.json` every minute. |
