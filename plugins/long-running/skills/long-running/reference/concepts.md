# Concepts

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| Order | One thing he asked for, in his words verbatim, because a paraphrase is not the spec. It holds a state, a kind, its parts, its jobs, its proof that it works where he uses it, its project and workstream, an optional due time, when a post promised him his next line (`drive order set <o> --due`), and its owner, the session that adds its jobs, starts its agents and posts its reports: `drive job add` and the Agent hook refuse the other session, naming the owner's session id to send a record to, and `say` and `drive order posted` refuse the other session's report on it, naming the report to pass: on the communicator's own order, one its handler posts from the communicator's lane. Kind `answer` is one reader's research and answer; `build` puts an Opus reader's research before any builder or designer. A part is one deliverable his words name, such as each stack of a design or each question; keep a live job or an unanswered card on every part until it is delivered, so he never has to remind the drive. Its jobs are those it started and those carrying one of its parts. The one order not in his words is drive tooling, a fix to a tool the drive runs on. Its words open `Drive tooling:`, the communicator owns it, it has no thread, its posts go at his DM root, and it carries no part of a feature order, so a feature's thread keeps one subject. `drive order …`, the `order` tool. |
| Job | One background agent owning one deliverable, started by one order. When a later order asks for work a running job owns, that job carries it, `drive job set <j> --carries <o> [--part "<part>"]`, listed under both orders as that part's live job until done. `long-running:builder`: one step of one change, the code and its open PR, or one event on that PR through a handoff, up to installed and proven; while design cards of his are open, the recommended option on a pushed branch, in state `built` with `--branch` and a `--waits` on each card, and outside an incident's repair `drive pr add` refuses its PR until he answers every card. `long-running:reader`: one question an order needs, read only, on Opus, `model: "sonnet"` only for a one-fact lookup. `long-running:designer`: the initial design of a tricky part and its questions for him, read only, on Fable, by [Design first](design.md). `codex:codex-wrapper`: review, diagnosis, incidents. The communicator's `long-running:handler`s, one per message of his, are not jobs, except one answering a read-only question, which the communicator records as its answer order's `reader` job so the watch holds the answer to him. Over this Mac's load limit (`drive place`), a monorepo builder starts on a Sprite with `--remote`. A job returns one line and keeps the rest with `drive job report <j> -`. [Jobs](jobs.md) lists the hooks that refuse other shapes. `drive job …`, the `job` tool. |
| Event | One line from a watch, the drive's own or a sub-coordinator's for its project, each a process of its own, a PR's without its title. `PR …` says a tracked PR changed, or sat 30 minutes red, green and approved with no card drawn, or green, approved, exempt and not landed; `TAP …`, he tapped Approve or Hold on a PR card; `FREE …`, the last card, job or PR a job or PR waits on is answered, done or merged; `WAITING-ON-HIM …`, a card of his started holding a job built on its recommended option, and the watch replied to him once, in plain words, in that card's own thread; `JOB …`, a remote lane reported or stopped; `LANE … died`, a remote lane's worker died; `DUE …`, a promised time passed with no post in its order's thread; `DUE j<n> …`, once, a job's clock ran out while it still runs; `LIVE o<n> …`, once, every release an open incident order waits on shipped its fix; `CARD … settled in place`, every PR his open PR card carries merged or closed; `ANSWER …`, an answer of his leaves cards or PRs unmarked; `ORPHAN …`, a part of an open order has nothing carrying it, one no job took once its order sat still five minutes, or its job is stuck, gone, or has an untracked open PR; `TICK …`, every 30 minutes while he is here, the orders with news or a promised time close; `EVENT catch-up …`, he is back. The watch also says `EVENT unheard …`, `EVENT refused …` and `WATCH …`. A `Drive tooling:` order's lines go to the communicator, a PR's move to the remote lane of the job waiting on it, and the rest over cci to the coordinator session hearing the order, the sub-coordinator bound for its project or else the top: `TAP`, `FREE`, `DUE`, `LIVE`, `ORPHAN`, `ANSWER`, `JOB`, `LANE`, catch-up, unheard and `WATCH stopped` lines and a PR's line whose job is not running wake it at once, the rest at most every 5 minutes; a PR's own new head, checks passed or approval wakes it only when the PR then waits on his card or on its landing as an exempt class. `drive manual coordinator <kind>` prints when each repeats and what to do. |
| Record | A cci record on the drive between the two sessions, one line with the rest in its body, by [Between the two sessions](records.md). |
| Message | A post to him in Slack, in a shape from [the messages page](messages.md): mainly an ack or summary in his thread, a question card at his DM root, or a PR card that cc-slack draws from the PRs, one per stack of at most four PRs, which `drive pr set --serves` and every card draw enforce. |
| Answer | His reply to a card or a question; a ruling is a standing direction of his. `drive answer` records each verbatim in cc-notes and marks every open card and PR his words may touch as standing, superseded or reopened. His words under a card answer it, and a question they leave open goes on a new card. `drive job card` refuses to redraw a card his words already answered, and `drive job ask` refuses a question a recorded answer of his may settle. A ruling binds only what his words name, and his feedback on a post or card names the library, `cc-slack check-post` and drive tools that let it through, by [His words](../SKILL.md#his-words). |
| Status | One model of where the drive stands, `drive view`, drawn by the dashboard and the Slack status page. First what waits on him, by priority: his open question cards, those holding up the most green approved PRs and built branches first, each saying how many; then PRs ready for his tap; then promised times that passed. Then each feature by state, naming the open question cards blocking it and when each was posted, and a built feature its branch. `drive dashboard` serves four screens: Your queue, numbered as the Slack page numbers it, each card drawn whole with the features it blocks; Now, open orders by project and workstream; Work, running and waiting jobs by project and workstream, flagging any quiet 45 minutes or more; History, each day newest first. Cmd-K jumps to any question, change, feature or job; a top strip shows only live incidents and how many questions and PR taps wait on him, since he asked to see only what he can act on. A pick, Approve or Hold there goes through `cc-slack card-answer`, so the Slack card, asking session and PR tap all hear a Slack tap; only this Mac or his own tailnet devices may answer. Words he types under a card reach his DM through `cc-slack dm-relay`, marked as typed on the dashboard, and the drive hears them as his DM. The page updates on each change to the drive's files and, in a secure context such as its https tailnet link or its loopback link on this Mac, notifies the browser of each new thing waiting on him. The watch also draws it as the drive's canvas tab in his DM and stores his open cards in `cards.json` every minute. |
