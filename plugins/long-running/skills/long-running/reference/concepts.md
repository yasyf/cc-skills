# Concepts

A glossary. What to do about each term is on the page the row links.

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| Order | One thing he asked for, in his words. It holds a state, a kind, its parts, its jobs, its proof that it works where he uses it, its project and workstream, a short title, an optional due time (`drive order set <o> --due`), and its owner: the session that adds its jobs, starts its agents, checks its PRs and posts its reports. `drive job add`, the Agent hook, `say` and `drive order posted` refuse another session and name the owner's session id. Kind `answer` is one reader's research and answer; kind `build` puts an Opus reader's research before any builder or designer. `drive order …`, the `order` tool. |
| Part | One deliverable of an order that his words name, such as each stack of a design, each question, or one old-tree port, `port of #N`, added by `drive job add --part`. A part of a build order or an incident, except an incident's first check and cause, is work: a builder, codex or design job carries it, and `drive job prove` delivers it once the proof file its newest builder or codex job keeps exits 0, writing that proof as the part's `posted`. A job a later job on the same part followed owes that part nothing. Every other part, and one `drive order set <o> --kind answer --part "<part>"` sorts as an answer, is words he reads, delivered by the drive's post whose report names it in its `part` attr or by the post a finished job's `POSTED <link>` return names. An answer order closes once every part is posted, every job on it finished, no due line is owed and every reporter heard. |
| Standing part | A lane that never ends, such as a test box or a census of ports, kept by `drive order set <o> --part "<part>" --standing "<goal>"`. It is not delivered, so its order stays open. Once no job on it is running, waiting or built, the watch starts the next pass, a codex job on this Mac on that goal, at most every 30 minutes, with a handoff quoting the last pass's return. The watch alone starts a pass, so `drive job launch` and `drive job resume` refuse a finished one. A pass's `DONE` or `HANDOFF` line reaches the coordinator as `evidence`. `drive status` and the dashboard list it with its live and last pass. |
| Drive tooling | The one order not in his words: how the drive itself works: a tool it runs on (the drive CLI and its MCP tools, a hook, the watch, the dashboard, cards and posts, cc-slack check-post, the drive's CI plumbing), this skill, its agents and role rules, how its sessions reach each other and him, and the install of a released tool. Its words open `Drive tooling:` and the meta session owns it, which every drive runs beside the communicator and the coordinator. It keeps the thread his words ordering it came in, else the thread its first post at his DM root starts. One a session opened that has posted nothing and has no teammate's report closes done on its proof with no line, through `drive job prove`, and shows only on the dashboard. It carries no part of a feature order, so a feature's thread keeps one subject. |
| Job | One background agent owning one deliverable, started by one order. It returns one line and keeps the rest with `drive job report <j> -`. When a later order asks for work a running job owns, that job carries it, `drive job set <j> --carries <o> [--part "<part>"]`: it is listed under both orders, its result counts on both, and its passing proof delivers the carried part. `long-running:builder`: one step of one change, the code and its open PR, or one event on that PR through a handoff, up to installed and proven; while design cards of his are open, the recommended option on a pushed branch, in state `built` with `--branch` and a `--waits` on each card. `long-running:reader`: one question an order needs, read only, on Opus, or `model: "sonnet"` for a one-fact lookup. `long-running:designer`: the design of a tricky part and its questions for him, read only, on Fable, by [Design first](design.md). A codex job is a codex-ask run `drive job launch` starts with no agent: review, diagnosis and incidents. Over this Mac's load limit (`drive place`), a monorepo builder whose brief has no `Mac:` or `Release:` line starts on a Sprite with `drive job launch`. [Jobs](jobs.md) lists the hooks. `drive job …`, the `job` tool. |
| Handler | A `long-running:handler` the communicator starts, one per message of his. It is not a job, except one answering a read-only question, which `drive handler` records as its answer order's `reader` job so the watch holds the answer to him. |
| Event | One line from a watch, the drive's own or a sub-coordinator's for its project, each a process of its own. The lines are listed under [Event lines](#event-lines). `drive manual coordinator <kind>` prints when each repeats and what to do. |
| Record | A cci record on the drive between its sessions, one line with the rest in its body, by [Between the sessions](records.md). |
| Message | A post to him in Slack, in a shape from [the messages page](messages.md): an ack or summary in his thread, a question card in that thread or at his DM root for an order with no thread, or a PR card. |
| PR card | One card per stack, drawn by cc-slack, whatever the stack's size; PR sections past the 50 blocks one Slack message holds go under its Explain more. The stack is one rule, `state.card_unit`, which the watch's `ready` record uses too: a PR's native GitHub stack, the one `stack-enqueue` lands, read on every draw; else the PRs its base branch chains it to; else the PRs asked with it of its repository that serve its order, so sibling PRs ready together draw one card and his Approve covers each. A PR added to a stack after its card went up joins that card, redrawn in place. A first card draws once no PR of the stack entered uncarded for 10 minutes, `stage.STACK_QUIET`, or one passed its 30-minute deadline. |
| Answer | His reply to a card or a question. A ruling is a standing direction of his. `drive answer`, or the record tool for a ruling its record names, records each in his words in cc-notes and marks every open card, undelivered part and PR his words may touch as standing, settled, dropped, superseded or reopened. It lists those of the answered card's job and order, of an order a lifted pick binds, and those his words name, one of another order marked by its key. His words under a card answer it, and `drive job card` refuses to redraw a card they answered. What `drive job ask` settles on is in [Scope](scope.md#a-question-for-him). His feedback on a post or card names the library, `cc-slack check-post` and drive tools that let it through, by [His words](../SKILL.md#his-words). |
| Repo rules | A rule a repository's own people set for every drive working in it, such as how a session claims a shared staging environment: a `[[rule]]` with an `id` and its `text` in that repository's `.claude/long-running.toml`. It is not a ruling of his, which binds his drive alone. Every role block and brief of a drive working there carries them from the drive's checkout, which the drive moves to its default branch's head before each launch, so a merge is what changes them. |
| Repo names | `<ci>`, `<guarded org>`, `<monorepo>`, `<pr scripts>`, `<staging env>`, `<sandbox env>`, `<prod env>` and `<emergency env>` in these pages stand for the repository's CI and release tool, the owner whose repositories land on his tap, the repository the drive releases from, its PR scripts, and its staging, sandbox, production and emergency envs. A `[[rule]]` of its `.claude/long-running.toml` gives each its name there, and each `[[page]]` table of it, a `page` and its `text`, is what `drive manual <page>` prints under `## In this repository` after that page. |
| Status | One model of where the drive stands, `drive view`, drawn by the dashboard and the Slack status page. First what waits on him: his open question cards, those holding up the most green approved PRs and built branches first, each saying how many; then PRs ready for his tap, each at the stage `stage.of` names now; then due times that passed. Then each feature by state, naming the open question cards blocking it and when each was posted, and a built feature its branch. The watch also draws it as the drive's canvas tab in his DM and stores his open cards in `cards.json` every minute. |
| Dashboard | `drive dashboard` serves five screens. Your queue: his cards and PRs in the Slack page's order, each card drawn whole with the features it blocks and a story Claude writes for it in the background, kept in the drive's `stories` folder; a card he typed words under sinks until the drive next posts in its thread. Now: open orders by project and workstream on a zoomable canvas, beside what waits on him there and the newest records naming it. Work: running and waiting jobs, flagging any quiet 45 minutes or more. History: each day, newest first. Pages: the live pages page jobs built, by [Pages](pages.md). A top strip shows live incidents, open bug reports and how many questions and PR taps wait on him; a bug report counts until every reporter is told the fix is live, at one of the stages reported, being fixed, merged, live and not told, or told. A pick, Approve or Hold there goes through `cc-slack card-answer`, so the Slack card, the asking session and the lander hear it as a Slack tap, and one on a PR with no card is recorded as his tap from the dashboard. This Mac and his own tailnet devices may answer. Words he types under a card reach his DM through `cc-slack dm-relay`, marked as typed on the dashboard, and the drive keeps an `asked` event naming the card. |

## Event lines

A PR's line comes without its title.

| Line | What it says |
|---|---|
| `PR …` | A tracked PR changed; Trunk ejected it from the merge queue; GitHub refused the merge Trunk's queue took; the drive's lander queued, merged or failed to land it; or it sat 30 minutes red, green and approved with no card drawn, or green, approved, exempt and not landed. |
| `TAP …` | He tapped Approve or Hold on a PR card. `TAP Approve … not landed …`: a PR he approved has had no lander for 10 minutes, naming why. |
| `FREE …` | The last card, job or PR a job or PR waits on is answered, done or merged, or a job a job waits on returned `BUILT`, named with its branch and head. |
| `WAITING-ON-HIM …` | A card of his started holding a job built on its recommended option, and the watch replied to him once in that card's thread. |
| `JOB …` | A job returned, wherever it runs, or a remote lane stopped. A Claude agent on this Mac has no `JOB` line: its result reaches the session that started it as its Agent result. |
| `LANE … died` | A remote lane's worker died, or a running job's agent on this Mac stopped on an API error, and the watch relaunched the worker that pass. |
| `LANE … stopped by its model's safeguards` | The watch set a running job stopped whose agent or lane a model's safeguards stopped, restarted nothing and passed Claude Code's notice to him. |
| `DUE …` | The time set on an order with no thread passed, said once. An order with a thread gets no `DUE`: the watch writes the report owing its line and starts the handler that posts it every 10 minutes until a post is there. An open incident has no time: the watch owes its next line five quiet minutes after its last once it has news. |
| `DUE j<n> …` | Once, a job's clock ran out while it still runs. |
| `DUE o<n> claim …` | Every 10 minutes from the end a post at a channel's root, such as a staging claim, named with `--attr until`, while no drive post is in its thread since. |
| `RECOVERED o<n> …` | An open incident is over: every merged fix PR of it is live in production, any monitor of it OK and each order of it that builds something he uses proven from that surface. The watch sent its step 8 report, which closes it. |
| `PROVE o<n> …` | Once, an open incident's fix is live but an order of it that builds something he uses keeps no recording or live post from there; it names the builder to add that proves it. |
| `TELL o<n> …` | Once, an order's fix is proven live, or an answer order's answer is posted, and a reporter's thread has no note 10 minutes on. |
| `CARD … settled in place` | Every PR his open PR card carries merged or closed. |
| `ANSWER …` | An answer of his leaves cards or PRs unmarked. |
| `ORPHAN …` | A part of an open order has nothing carrying it: no job took it once its order sat still five minutes, or its job is stuck, gone or has an untracked open PR. |
| `EVENT refused …`, `WATCH …` | The watch's own lines. |
| `LANDER …` | The lander's own failure, its `stack-enqueue` exiting 5, which goes to the meta session alone as a defect. |

Where a line goes: a `Drive tooling:` order's lines go to the meta session, a PR's move to the remote
lane of the job waiting on it, and the rest over cci to the coordinator session hearing the order
(a job's own order, not one it only carries), the sub-coordinator bound for its project or else the
top. `TAP`, `FREE`, `DUE`, `TELL`, `PROVE`, `ORPHAN`, `ANSWER`, `JOB`, `LANE` and `WATCH stopped`
lines, and a PR's line whose job is not running, wake it at once. The rest wake it at most every 5
minutes, the lines one stage of a pass makes for a session going as one record whose text counts
them by first word and whose body holds each. A PR's own new head, passed checks, approval or
`queued for merge` wakes it only when the PR then waits on its landing as an exempt class. His
`TAP Approve` of a `<monorepo>` PR wakes it only when the PR is red or conflicts, or no lander has
taken it for 10 minutes, since the lander queues it otherwise.
