# Concepts

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| Order | One thing he asked for: his words verbatim, a state, an optional due time, its parts, its jobs, its proof. A part is one deliverable his words name, such as each stack of a design or each question; every part has a live job or a card he has yet to answer, until it is delivered. Its jobs are those it started and those carrying one of its parts. The one order not in his words is drive tooling, a fix to a tool the drive runs on: its words open `Drive tooling:`, it has no thread, its lines go at his DM root, and it never carries a part of his feature order. `drive order …` |
| Job | One background agent owning one deliverable, started by one order. When a later order of his asks for work a running job already owns, that job carries the later order's part too, `drive job set <j> --carries <o> [--part "<part>"]`, listed under both orders and counted as that part's live job until the job is done: `long-running:builder` (one step of one change: the code and its open PR, or one event on that PR through a handoff, up to installed and proven; or, while design cards of his are open, the recommended option on a pushed branch and no PR, a job in state `built` that `--waits` on each card until he answers), `long-running:reader` (one question an order needs, read only, on Sonnet), `long-running:designer` (the initial design of a part with a tricky design and its questions for him, read only, on Fable, by [Design first](design.md)), `codex:codex-wrapper` (review, diagnosis, incidents). The communicator's `long-running:handler`s, one per message of his, are not jobs. A monorepo builder started while this Mac is over its load limit is a remote lane on a Sprite instead. A job returns one line and keeps the rest of its return with `drive job report`. `drive job …` |
| Event | A line from `drive watch`: `PR …` state changes, `PR … still red` or `… with no card drawn` once a PR has held that 30 minutes and again only when its head, checks, review, tap, job or what it `--waits` on changes, with no card owed while a wait holds, `FREE …` once when the last card or job a job or PR `--waits` on is answered or done, `TAP …` on his PR cards, `JOB …` reports from remote lanes, `LANE … died` once when a remote lane's worker dies, `DUE …` from a promised time, every 10 minutes, until a drive post is in its order's thread, `ORPHAN …` the moment a part of an open order has nothing carrying it, or a running job's agent or lane finished or ended with no result recorded, or has an open PR the drive does not track, `TICK …` every 30 minutes while he is here, naming the orders with news since their last line or a promised time close, `EVENT catch-up …` when he is back. Each is one line, a PR's without its title; `TAP`, `DUE`, `JOB`, `LANE`, catch-up and unheard lines come at once, and the rest are held and come together at most every 5 minutes. |
| Record | A cci record on the drive between the two sessions, one line with the rest in its body, by [Between the two sessions](records.md). |
| Message | An ack or a summary line in his thread, a question card at his DM root, or a PR card, one per stack, that cc-slack draws from the PRs. Nothing else. [reference/messages.md](messages.md) |
| Status | One model of where the drive stands, `drive view`: what waits on him, highest priority first (his open question cards, those holding up the most green approved PRs and built branches first, each saying how many, then PRs ready for his tap, then promised times that passed), then each feature by state (blocked, running, built, waiting, merged, live, not started), each naming the open question cards of his that block it and when each was posted, and a built feature its branch. `drive dashboard` serves it as seven screens, Your queue (each card numbered by its place in the whole queue, as the Slack page numbers it, and drawn whole, the same card Slack draws, naming the features it blocks, and answered in place: a pick or Approve/Hold goes through `cc-slack card-answer`, so the Slack card, the asking session and the PR's tap all hear it as a Slack tap; only this Mac or his own tailnet devices may answer; words he types under a card go through `cc-slack dm-relay`, which posts them in his DM marked as typed on the dashboard and routes them to the drive as his DM), Now (what needs him, one sentence on where the drive stands, what is blocked and what is moving), Status (overall progress, each feature's PR bar and its own page with his words, jobs and PRs), Review (every open PR, waiting on him, not ready yet, or approved or held, each with a box that tells Claude what to change, and each opening in place with its description, its diff and the Slack thread under its card, where he asks about the change and reads the answer as it comes back), Chat (every ask of his, with its Slack thread drawn on the page, any card in it answerable there, a reply box and Got it under it, and a box for a new ask), Work (jobs grouped as quiet 45 minutes or more, running, built, waiting and finished in the last day) and History (the last day), with a jump palette (Cmd-K) over every question, change, feature and job, an alerts bar for quiet jobs, passed promises and red PRs, and a browser notification for each new thing that waits on him wherever the page is a secure context (its https link on the tailnet name, served with the machine's Tailscale certificate when the tailnet issues them, and its loopback link on this Mac), pushed to the page on each change to the drive's files, and `drive watch` draws it as the drive's canvas tab in his DM and stores his open cards in the drive's `cards.json` every minute. |
