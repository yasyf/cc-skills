# Scope, and asking him

Every PR the drive touches is one of four things, decided before any builder starts on it and said in its brief's `Serves` line, its builder's `READY`, its `ready` record and every summary line about it, so work outside scope is caught before it starts; the `agent_brief` hook denies a `long-running:builder` whose brief has no `Serves:` line and no `Handoff:` line.

- **A port**, "port of #N": a change carried between the old tree and the new one with no behaviour beyond what the target tree needs to run #N's. Its title opens `Port:`, and it is called "port of #N" wherever he sees it. The coordinator checks it against #N's diff and records `drive pr set <url> --port-of <N>`, which sets the `pure port` exemption with no tap (`drive pr set` refuses `--tap` with `--port-of`); a handoff builder lands it and posts "port of #N landed" in its order's thread, like every exempt class in [reference/landing.md](landing.md).
- **His order**: his message or card pick asking for this build, quoted wherever the PR is named to him. Record it with `drive pr set <url> --tap --serves <o>`; `drive pr set` refuses `--tap` without `--serves`. An order for cards or a summary is delivered by those cards and licenses none of the fixes until he picks one. Register a PR under an order only when its words ask for that build, never because jobs it unblocks serve that order. A defect in something the drive shipped or is building belongs to the order that shipped it and names that order in its line.
- **Drive tooling**: a fix to a tool the drive runs on or a skill rewrite his ruling asks for, by [Tooling](../SKILL.md#tooling), fixed without asking under its own order. The communicator adds `drive order add "Drive tooling: <what it fixes>" --from cci:<seq>` with no `--thread` and runs its builder; `drive order add` refuses a "Drive tooling:" order from the coordinator's session or with `--thread`, `drive job add` refuses a job on it from the coordinator's session, and `drive pr set --serves <o>` and `drive job set --carries <o>` refuse mixing a "Drive tooling:" order with another order. Its PR's His words list says none, and its lines go at his DM root, never in a feature thread. A `yasyf/*` PR lands with no tap; a `Forge-AI/*` PR lands under an exempt class or serves its own order with `drive pr set <url> --tap --serves <its Drive tooling order> --design none` for a PR card at his DM root quoting "Drive tooling: …" above it.
- **Neither**: the drive does not build, polish or card it. Return another lane's PR or bug report in a `handoff` record. Turn an unordered finding from a sweep, teammate or job into a question card summarizing what was found, and build nothing before his pick.

Ask only what is his to decide—architecture and abstractions, scope, and his workflow—and do everything else within his orders (ports, closes, restarts, backfills, CI fixes, cleanups, tool fixes), reporting the result with a count.

- Never ask a question he has already answered. `drive job ask` and every `ask` record check the card against his recorded answers and the whole block against the drive's answers, refusing with each answer that may settle it; follow any answer that settles the question or names the service to build, or pass `--unsettled "1396ce1: <why>; 729fb9a: <why>"` (or the record's `unsettled` attr) when none does.
- Wait for his word before crossing a trust line: landing a non-exempt `Forge-AI/*` change ([reference/landing.md](landing.md)), posting as him to others, changing production, or stopping or closing sessions he did not name. His order to post in his DM, a terminal or a card tap is that word for that one post; for an unordered post to a reporter or teammate, send a question card asking whether to tell them, never a card to approve a post.
- Send a design question to him before any PR carries it, by [Design first](design.md). A job may build the recommended option on a pushed branch meanwhile, but `drive job set` and `drive pr add` refuse opening a PR while design cards remain open.
- A question card is the job's `ASK` kept by `drive job ask`, which checks its shape and that each option lands releasable alone by [Land a change](landing.md#every-pr) with no post-release fill, backfill, pass or command. It holds the question; a one-to-three-line summary of what was found, what is decided, the recommendation, and what happens meanwhile, linking each openable item; a Show me with a complete worked example in the fitting visual; and two to four one-line options stating what he gets and what it costs. A question card's options come from the job's own evidence: the fix its diagnosis points at is always one of them, and it is the recommended one unless the summary says why not. `drive job ask` refuses a block whose findings carry no `Direct fix:` line naming that option by its words, or `Direct fix: none: <why>` when no diagnosis points at one. Keep findings in the job's file for Explain more rather than a separate post.
- His answer is the option he tapped or his written choice of a single option; if his written reply names no single option, ask him again under the card rather than mapping it to the closest option, and never offer an option that asks a reporter or anyone else to retry or confirm.
